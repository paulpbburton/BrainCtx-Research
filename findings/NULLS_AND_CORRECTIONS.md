The readout corpus — including what did not work and what had to be
retracted — is the research product. This page is the current-era record;
the ablation-era nulls (the sealed null primary endpoint; the
zero-voluntary-asks demonstration) stand in the
[archive](../archive/README.md).

## Nulls

- **Retrieval vocabulary.** In the need-first delivery rounds, most early
  typed needs found no knowledge-base answer — not because the knowledge
  was absent, but because the asker's vocabulary did not bridge to the
  stored rule's. Accumulating askers' phrasings onto the lesson rows moved
  replayed recall substantially (a circular measure by construction, and
  said so); a stemming/synonym retriever remains open. (Anchor: `NULL-02`)
- **Founder-class questions mostly unanswerable from the store.**
  Questions about operator rulings — the highest-value class — largely
  could not be answered from the knowledge base in the measured rounds.
  Recorded as the gap it is.
- **The unexercised exemption.** A justified-shape branch added to a
  sweep classifier was measured by its own review round to excuse zero
  rows while structurally reproducing a paid-for relapse archetype; it was
  removed the same day, with the re-entry condition (a real specimen WITH
  a pinning fixture) written where it died. Its sibling exemption now
  stands unexercised in the live window — named, not hidden.

## Corrections

- **A denominator claim withdrawn.** A report's historical active/inactive
  split and liveness count had no retained time-indexed source; the
  numbers were withdrawn in writing and the axes re-typed `UNAVAILABLE`.
  The narrower reproducible observation replaced them. (Anchor: `CORR-01`)
- **A misattribution reversed.** An episode initially read as a delivery
  routing defect was corrected: the cause was host-interface session
  switching, not the instrument; the operator ruled no instrument changes
  from it, and the wrongly applied edit sweep was reverted byte-exact.
  (Anchor: `CORR-02`)
- **The as-built corrected against itself.** Two facts in the
  instrument's public-facing as-built (its delivery event set; its
  pre-action entrypoint) were wrong in first draft, caught by independent
  review, and the document now records that its verification stamp had
  covered them falsely — the stamp was replaced with the actual reading
  basis. (Anchor: `CORR-03`)
- **A review verdict token outside the contract.** A dispatched reviewer
  was offered a trajectory token the review contract does not permit; the
  mechanical gate blocked, and the reviewer itself restated its judgment
  in the permitted vocabulary as an append-only correction — reviewer
  independence preserved through the fix. (Anchor: `REVIEW-01`)

- **Natural-work cases carrying their own adversity** (see the
  [case ledger](NATURAL_CODING_WORK_SPECIMENS.md)): the burden case where
  the observer's design produced the timeout wall it then had to be
  repaired for (`CX-BURDEN-01`); the post-merge defect behind an
  initially merged repair (`CX-REPAIR-01`); the refused-rather-than-
  estimated denominator split (`CX-DENOM-01`, the natural-work narrative
  of `CORR-01`); and a case-card claim dropped at roster freeze when the
  custodian found the "unrecoverable" intervention turns retained in
  custody — the freeze process correcting the originating publication proposal it froze.

The pattern across all of these: the failure surfaces loudly, the
correction is append-only and attributed, and the corrected claim is
narrower than the original. That discipline — not the absence of error —
is what this page evidences.
