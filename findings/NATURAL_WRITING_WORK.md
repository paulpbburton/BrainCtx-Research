## The guarded writing loop (as designed and as observed)

The loop under observation: before the writing asserts a checkable
fact, the writer files a typed need; the knowledge base answers first
(with scored, cited references); a miss speaks exactly once with a
typed remedy; a research-genre need commissions a bounded world-tier
seat whose evidence card folds back into the brain; and a claim neither
the brain nor bounded research can settle is preserved as an explicit
unknown instead of being written around. The design intent, in the
retained record's own words, is that a prospective unsupported claim
"first consults the relevant project knowledge base, then performs
bounded current-world research when the knowledge base cannot settle
it, and otherwise preserves an explicit unknown rather than emitting
unsupported content." (Anchor: `NW-GUARD-01`)

## First specimens (retained; each a single observation)

- **The guard was designed by the workload itself.** The writing
  program's own typed need commissioned the research that specified the
  KB-first-then-world fabrication guard above — the writing workload
  asking the instrument to constrain it. (Anchor: `NW-GUARD-01`)
- **A day of guarded self-audit.** In one observed afternoon the
  writing program filed ten knowledge-only audit needs against its own
  staged artifact (consistency, link integrity, stale-language,
  fresh-reader review). One was answered from a page the lane had
  ingested that same morning; nine misses each produced one typed note,
  witnessed and receipted in the writer's ledger. The audits were
  answered by inspection, not delegated — the correct disposition for
  self-referential questions, and the observation that shaped the
  lane's three-arm remedy. (Anchor: `NW-LOOP-01`)
- **The guard rejected its own instrument's weak answer.** One retained
  receipt shows the writer refusing a scored knowledge-base match with
  an extractive reason — the cited section was "topical overlap ...
  not an answer to the corrected need" — and narrowing its claim
  instead of citing a wrong page. Judgment against the lane, exercised
  by its consumer, retained. (Anchor: `NW-LOOP-01`)
- **The failure mode is already public.** The same writing program is
  the workload that exposed the silenced-lane defect carded as RC-02 on
  the case ledger: with the lane muted, the guarded loop produced no
  brain activity at all — the strongest evidence that the guard, when
  silenced, fails toward unguarded writing rather than toward silence
  the writer notices. (Anchor: `RC-LANE-01`, carded there)

## What this page will accrue
