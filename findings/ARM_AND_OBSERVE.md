# Finding — arm and observe: the tuning flywheel

> **Authority note:** The private record is authoritative; this repository is the legible surface.
> **Status:** descriptive finding · **As of:** 2026-09-01 · Force and
> ceilings: [../STATUS_AND_CLAIMS.md](../STATUS_AND_CLAIMS.md) governs.

The operator's ruling that created this practice: *start arming a pattern
and observing fires rather than hoping we happened to capture historical
depth — every fire is a tuning specimen.* Five deterministic predicates
were armed for the operator's most recurrent failure classes (thrashing
reviews, doctrine-drift signatures, goal displaced by literal words,
research retained but unused, decisions anchored after the acts they
govern), plus a pre-action twin for the decision-lag class.

**The measured period.** Roughly thirty receipted fires on natural
workload in a few days. True positives changed real work: a third
re-spelling of an established rule became a shared library; a
late-anchoring habit reversed into anchor-before-push; a thrashing review
thread was forced down to a minimal corrective change instead of a fifth
argument. (Anchor: `FLYWHEEL-01`)

**The false fires are the point.** Every identified false-fire class
became a same-day predicate fix that held on its next natural
re-exercise, among them: harness-generated notifications read as operator
messages; quoted command text inside a receipt matched as an executed
act; a quoted verdict above the review's actual final verdict; a held,
never-executed intent counted as an act (still open — the
intent-without-result discriminator is owed); and sound records invisible
under a nonconforming heading, fixed by making the fire text name the
exact required form.

**The receipt discipline.** Every delivered fire asks the actor for three
typed lines (disposition, claim correctness, basis completeness) plus a
three-column tuning note: what the fire got right, what it got wrong, and
what would have distinguished it. Positives are recorded with equal
standing — the record is the product, never overhead. A receipt is
handling evidence, never causal usefulness.

**Ceilings.** Fire counts from one period under evolving predicates are
tuning specimens, not a precision estimate; semantic adjudication of raw
matches and per-case causal help are unbuilt. The custody that makes these
numbers checkable — pre-delivery journaling, six-stage episode projection,
typed denominators — is described in
[../ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). (Anchors: `FLYWHEEL-01`,
`EP-01`, `DEN-01`)

## A second measured period (fresh pass, 2026-08-29)

One intensive build week later, a fresh pass over the suite's recent
work — measured from one orchestrating session's complete receipt
ledger (95 receipted deliveries) plus the week's review rounds; the
single-session denominator is the stated ceiling. (Anchor:
`FLYWHEEL-02`)

**Where the suite earned its keep this week was the review harness,
not the reminders.** Across two merged changes to the research
mechanism itself, the paired independent seats caught, by measurement:
a fold-back recipe copied from a stale surface that would have
delivered a WRONG process authoritatively to every future recipient,
one-shot and uncorrectable; a completeness predicate that would have
routed zero of 184 live-shaped candidates as researchable — the
instrument silently re-silencing the very lane the operator had just
un-muzzled, one commit after the un-muzzling; a merge-day adoption hole
under which a live runaway lineage (94 open machine-filed rows, depth
frontier 13, growing during the review itself) would have restarted as
fresh roots; a test that passed vacuously on an empty list — the only
check asserting the mechanism still worked; and three stops that stayed
green when inverted. Every catch carried its measurement, and two
were made by re-measuring the LIVE stores rather than the fixtures.

**The armed reminders, receipted:** the largest class (62 equipoise
items) split 33 used / 21 partially used / 7 rejected-with-reason /
1 deferred. The pre-action decision-ordering entry ran 6 used against
16 rejected — every rejection the SAME false-fire shape: the join
compared against a stale index and fired on a window an existing
conforming record had already closed (once 22 seconds after the anchor
it failed to see). That shape is this period's counterpart of the
first period's harness-notification false fires: identified, receipted
with the distinguishing predicate each time, fix not yet landed —
carried openly. Across all receipted deliveries the actor-declared
effect split: 12 changed the work, 7 provoked a check that found
something, 7 provoked a check that found nothing, 53 confirmed
something already known, 9 no effect or nothing to do.

**The pause-once class operated as designed and exposed its tuning
need.** Single-shot pre-action pauses held five world-contact acts this
week (publish pushes, a subagent dispatch, whole-file writes); every
one was re-issued after the pause with the context weighed, every one
receipted `PROCEEDED_CORRECT` — a 100% proceed rate on this period's
population, which is not evidence the class is useless (the record
shows the deciding happened with the memory in hand) but is exactly
the population statistic that argues for the receipted improvement:
publish flows the operator has standingly authorized should be
allow-listed rather than re-paused. (Anchor: `FLYWHEEL-02`)

**Ceilings for this pass.** One session's ledger dominates the receipt
population; dispositions are declared by the supervised actor itself
(the honesty instrument here is the typed vocabulary and the retained
ledger, not independent adjudication); review-harness catches are
enumerated, not sampled; and no counterfactual exists for any of it.

## A third measured period (fleet-wide pass, 2026-08-30 → 08-31)

The first period measured one predicate family; the second, one
orchestrating session's ledger. This pass is the first fleet-wide
window: roughly thirty hours of natural workload, measured from every
session ledger the supervisor touched, extracted by delegated read-only
sweeps and re-produced by the publishing session's own independent runs
before release. (Anchor: `FLYWHEEL-03`)

**Steady state.** The hub ran the whole window on a single process more
than five days old — zero restarts, an empty service journal, roughly
3,200 heartbeats at held cadence, and no new failure instances filed.
The only error stream was a single armed pattern's durable-evaluation
conflict re-emitted every tick (~19,000 times) — one known defect
carried openly; the volume argues a one-time cleanup of its conflicting
rows rather than any predicate change.

**The receipt population.** 207 typed handling receipts across 30
distinct session ledgers — the receipt vocabulary is now fleet
behavior, not one session's discipline. Dispositions: 121 used, 45
partially used, 30 rejected with reason, 11 others (deferred,
hand-raise, remaining-gap, and a few blocks that mixed need vocabulary
into receipt form — recorded as found). Actor-adjudicated correctness:
129 correct, 43 partly correct, 19 incorrect, 11 undecidable in the
actor's aperture. One in five nudges reasoned from a window missing
evidence of what the actor had actually done — typed separately from
correctness exactly so a capture defect cannot be misread as the
review being wrong. Effect: 74 changed the work, 74 confirmed
something already known, 34 provoked a check (19 of which found
something), 20 reported no effect or nothing to do; a handful of
blocks omit a typed line and stand as written.

**This period's false-fire shape.** Dominant among the thirty
rejections: the supervisor asserting an absence that the window's own
compound command already disproved — probes and their outputs living
inside multi-part shell calls the join did not look inside. The first
period's shape was harness notifications read as operator messages;
the second's, a stale index; this one is the compound-command
aperture. Two lesser shapes: cards delivered against a different
requester's need, and anchor-ordering alarms on commits that were in
fact anchored. Each rejection carries its distinguishing predicate in
the receipt.

**Guards, pauses, and the research lane.** 90 guard reminders
(anchor-before-world-contact and overwrite-without-reading at 30 each
— two-thirds of the total), 41 single-shot pre-action pauses, most
claimed. The research lane ran 130 ticks: 81 fresh commissions, 80
delivered and ingested cards, 15 knowledge-base answers, 37 filed miss
notes, zero delivery refusals, one seat-spawn failure retained with
its cursor for retry.

**Ceilings for this pass.** One ~thirty-hour window under one

## A fourth measured period (overnight interval, 2026-08-31 → 09-01)

A ~17-hour follow-on interval, measured the same way — delegated
read-only sweeps plus the publishing session's crossed re-runs. Where
the two independent extractors' parse boundaries disagreed (one to two
receipt blocks, a timestamp-normalization edge), the difference is
disclosed rather than averaged; the publishing session's run is the one
printed. (Anchor: `FLYWHEEL-04`)

**Steady state, and a correction.** Six days on one hub process, an
empty journal, ~1,820 heartbeats. The single durable-evaluation
conflict kept emitting (~10,900 more re-emissions at ~650/hour) — the
cleanup is now owed, not merely argued. And a visible correction to the
third period: the seat-spawn failure described there as "retained with
its cursor for retry" was never retried — the interval shows no retry
event for that batch, only fresh batches succeeding around it (18 of
18 spawned and verified). The cursor-retention half of the claim stood;
the implied retry did not happen. (`CORR` on `FLYWHEEL-03`)

**The lane ran balanced.** 33 research ticks: 16 commissioned, 16
delivered, 16 ingested — nothing stranded; 8 knowledge-base answers, 2
miss notes, zero refusals. One receipted counter-specimen from the
publishing sessions themselves: on two consecutive days, a
window-scoped measurement need was offered the same timeless
program-summary page as a knowledge-base answer. A page whose as-of
predates the window's start can never settle a question about that
window; the distinguishing predicate is receipted for the tuning loop.

**The receipt population.** 46 disposition-bearing receipts across 8
session ledgers (45 typed, 1 malformed block recorded as found): 18
used, 18 partially used, 8 rejected with reason, 1 accountable
declination. Correctness: 19 correct, 16 partly, 9 incorrect, 1
undecidable. Basis: 19 of 45 reasoned from a window missing the
actor's actual work — roughly double the third period's one-in-five
capture-defect rate, on a much smaller population. Effect: 19 changed
the work, 14 confirmed something known, 10 provoked checks (4 found
something). This interval's rejection shape:
citation-does-not-contain-the-claimed-content — a nudge asserting a
cited section says something it does not.

**Guards.** 14 reminders (anchor-before-world-contact 9) and 10
pre-action pauses; 5 of the 9 anchor-class interrupts went unclaimed —
the first period-over-period claim-rate drop, flagged for watching.

**Ceilings.** A ~17-hour overnight interval with small populations;
actor-declared dispositions; extractor parse sensitivity disclosed
above; live counters read at stated times; no benefit rate, no
counterfactual. (Anchor: `FLYWHEEL-04`)
