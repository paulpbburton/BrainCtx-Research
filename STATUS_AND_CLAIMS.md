## Supportable claims

Each claim names its force and its anchor. An authorized reviewer resolves
anchors inside private custody via [`PRIVATE_ANCHORS.md`](PRIVATE_ANCHORS.md).

1. **An attachable supervision-and-memory instrument exists and operates.**
   A per-session component attaches through a coding-agent host's native
   hook surface (every hook event type wired, bounded per-event synchronous
   cost); a long-running hub observes attached sessions, evaluates five
   armed predicates per heartbeat, and delivers cited non-blocking nudges;
   the custody under it is append-only and strictly replayable. Force:
   demonstrated mechanism, verified against the installed system. (Anchor:
   `COMP-01`)
2. **The tuning flywheel ran on natural work.** In one measured period,
   roughly thirty receipted fires occurred on natural workload; true
   positives changed real work; every identified false-fire class became a
   same-day predicate fix that held on its next natural re-exercise. Force:
   retained descriptive measurement over one period; not a precision or
   benefit rate. (Anchor: `FLYWHEEL-01`)
3. **Typed research needs were answered into the asking session.** Over
   four measured rounds, delivery defects (wrong knowledge store, wrong
   session lane, page-level citation) were each fixed and re-exercised,
   ending at line-cited sections at a fraction of the earlier reading cost
   — alongside an honest null: most early needs found no knowledge-base
   answer because the asker's vocabulary did not bridge to the stored
   rule's. Force: mechanism-and-development result from an adaptive series;
   **not** a controlled superiority claim. (Anchors: `NEED-01`, `NULL-02`)
4. **Concern episodes, denominators, and addressability have strict
   custody.** Every terminal predicate evaluation journals its result
   before delivery; a projector joins fire-time sources, delivery,
   handling, and later correction into six-stage episodes verified by
   exact byte ranges; population, provenance, validity, and cost are
   independent typed axes where unavailable evidence reduces the
   conclusion rather than being inferred; lifetime identity custody is
   separated from operational addressability so hot work is proportional
   to the affirmatively active set. One denominator claim was found
   invalid in review and withdrawn in writing; the correction is part of
   the record. Force: demonstrated mechanism with bounded verified
   populations. (Anchors: `EP-01`, `DEN-01`, `LIFE-01`, `CORR-01`)
5. **The timing floor is measured.** A non-directive pre-action reminder
   lands too late to change its own act; act-shape matching pays a
   permanent self-reference price. This is why the ladder pairs a single
   exact-action pause with reminders aimed at the next act. Force:
   measured on the live pre-action plane. (Anchor: `TIMING-01`)
6. **A recurrence-ranked failure-mode characterization of an adversarial
   review corpus exists.** Roughly a thousand findings across ~170 review
   documents cluster into seventeen recurring modes; the top modes concern
   unverified byte-identity claims, overclaim without a reproducing
   artifact, vacuous tests, stale citations, and silently disabled signal.
   Force: descriptive characterization of a retained natural-work corpus;
   headline counts publish only with the public extraction receipt, which
   is pending. (Anchor: `REVIEW-CORPUS-01`)
7. **Paid-for lessons become deterministic checks.** The operating rule —
   a lesson that stays prose is the defect — is enacted: each entered
   lesson row carries its measured specimens and the predicate that now
   checks it on the instrument's own cadence. Force: demonstrated
   practice. (Anchor: `LESSON-01`)
8. **The adjacent-system position is explicit.** A concept-extraction pass
   over the closest public systems concedes non-novelty for observation,
   nudging, typed records, replay, and gates, adopts eleven narrow
   distinctions, rejects nine, and bounds the differentiation claim to the
   connected lifecycle. Force: synthesis over pinned public sources; not
   an efficacy comparison. (Anchor: `ADJ-01`)

## Ceilings — what this surface must not be read as claiming

- **No general causal benefit.** No claim here establishes that the
  instrument improved eventual results, accelerated work, or prevented
  failure. The counterfactual and per-case causal attribution are unbuilt.
- **No burden verdict.** Operator-attention and interruption burden over
  the long run are unmeasured; the instrument makes previously invisible
  activity reviewable, which is a different claim.
- **No precision rate.** Fire counts from one period under evolving
  predicates are tuning specimens, not a precision estimate.
- **No production readiness.** The instrument runs one operator's natural
  work on one host family; portability, hardening, and third-party
  deployment are not claimed.
- **Nulls are results.** The ablation-era nulls (archive) and the
  current-era retrieval nulls stand as findings, not as failures to be
  explained away.
