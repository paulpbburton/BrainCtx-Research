BrainCtx Research welcomes bounded collaboration around questions that can be
made empirically precise.

## Current needs

### Independent artifact evaluation

Review retained coding artifacts under a declared rubric without access to
private actor context. The goal is not endorsement; null, negative, mixed, and
regression findings are useful outcomes.

### Replication

Help translate the knowledge-to-action measurement into another consequential
coding workflow while preserving task, exposure, and evaluation boundaries.

### Research-method review

Challenge study populations, counterfactuals, leakage controls, claim status,
and the separation between mechanism evidence and benefit evidence.

### Compute or API support

Support finite, predeclared study packages with retained call, time, cost, and
failure accounting.

### Design partners

Identify real workflows where coding agents:

- lose situated knowledge;
- accept weak work after green tests;
- require repeated human steering;
- or lack audit-ready evidence connecting context to decisions and outcomes.

### Roles and career conversations

Relevant areas include agent systems, evaluations, runtime oversight,
knowledge infrastructure, coding-agent reliability, and evidence-preserving
research engineering.

## Collaboration boundary

Collaboration does not imply access to the full private implementation,
transcripts, corpus, prompts, provider state, or unrestricted run evidence.
Any private evidence access requires a separately defined purpose, rights and
privacy review, and handling agreement.

The project will not change a frozen study after observing its result to make
the outcome more favorable.
