# Research highlights

> **Authority note:** The private record is authoritative; this repository is the legible surface.
> **Status:** the findings a reader should not leave without · **As of:** 2026-08-26
> Claim force and ceilings: [STATUS_AND_CLAIMS.md](STATUS_AND_CLAIMS.md) governs.

1. **Presence is not activation.** The ablation era's sealed result: with
   knowledge available, a live team announced, and relevant uncertainty
   written in the agents' own words, voluntary consultation was zero. The
   distinction between written uncertainty, available help, and actual
   consultation is the program's founding measured fact.
   ([archive](archive/README.md); anchors `ABL03-01`, `ABL04-DEMO-01`)

2. **Arm-and-observe beats mining history.** Rather than hoping historical
   depth was captured, the program armed deterministic detectors and let
   natural work supply the specimens. Every fire is a tuning specimen with
   a three-column note (what was right, what was wrong, what would have
   distinguished it); every false-fire class in the measured period became
   a same-day predicate fix that held on re-exercise. (Anchor: `FLYWHEEL-01`)

3. **The timing floor is real.** A non-directive reminder delivered at the
   moment of an act lands too late to change that act; and a predicate
   matched on act shape cannot tell performing X from describing X, a
   permanent self-reference price. The workable ladder is a single
   exact-action pause plus reminders aimed at the next act. (Anchor:
   `TIMING-01`)

4. **Vocabulary is the retrieval bottleneck.** When typed research needs
   were answered from the knowledge base, most early misses were not
   missing knowledge — the asker's vocabulary failed to bridge to the
   stored rule's. Accumulating askers' phrasings onto the stored lessons
   moved replayed recall substantially; a stemming/synonym retriever
   remains open. (Anchors: `NEED-01`, `NULL-02`)

5. **Instruments that measure nothing are the dominant defect class.**
   Across the program's own supervision defects, the failure was almost
   never a predicate's judgment — it was the dispatcher's reach: heartbeats
   through wedges, health fields never extended, claims resting on sources
   that never existed. The countermeasures — typed UNAVAILABLE over
   inferred values, denominators with member identities, claims that carry
   their reproducing command, withdrawn claims withdrawn in writing — are
   themselves findings. (Anchors: `CORR-01`, `LESSON-01`)

6. **Reviews stopped being theater when seeded on the relevant set.**
   Independent review seats seeded on the diff, its issue, the prior
   round, and the rule sections engaged — instead of a whole corpus —
   produced sharper findings at roughly a fifth of the reading, including
   findings against the implementing actor, on both the observed domain
   workload and the instrument's own repository. (Anchor: `REVIEW-01`)

7. **The lessons-become-checks discipline closes the loop.** A lesson that
   stays prose is the defect: each paid-for lesson carries its measured
   specimens and the deterministic predicate that now checks it live. The
   registry also lists lessons still awaiting predicates — owed work,
   visibly. (Anchor: `LESSON-01`)

8. **The apparatus can become the burden.** In the natural-work case
   ledger's strongest adverse specimen, the observer's own shared
   hot-file design produced a hook-timeout wall under sibling-lane
   fanout, and eight review rounds accumulated before the operator froze
   the working scope in-session — the supervision and review apparatus
   consuming the work it is meant to support. Its contrast case delivered
   with focused testing and required a post-merge correction of its own
   (the two cases are not comparable — different scope, goal, and code
   area).
   Neither direction carries a causal or prevalence claim.
   ([case ledger](findings/NATURAL_CODING_WORK_SPECIMENS.md); anchors
   `CX-BURDEN-01`, `CX-REPAIR-01`)
