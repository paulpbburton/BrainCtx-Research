# Natural work — the observed workloads, by evidentiary role

> **Authority note:** The private record is authoritative; this repository is the legible surface.
> **Status:** descriptive evidence page · **As of:** 2026-08-26
> **Naming rule:** private workloads are described by type of work, never by
> name; only BrainCtx and this repository are named. This page is a
> rewrite, not a redaction — details that would identify private work are
> absent by construction, not blacked out.

This page is the workload-role overview; the episode/session case layer
beneath it — selected specimens with equal standing for adversity — is
the [natural coding-work case ledger](findings/NATURAL_CODING_WORK_SPECIMENS.md).

Almost none of the program's current-era evidence comes from synthetic
benchmarks. The supervision instrument was built while — and measured
against — real work in several private repositories. Their evidentiary
roles differ, and repository count is not a quality metric; the claim this
page supports is "related failure motifs and support needs were observed
across varied natural workloads," not statistical generalization.
(Anchor: `NW-01`)

| Workload (by type) | Role in the research |
|---|---|
| **BrainCtx itself** (the instrument's own repository) | Self-hosted development: the component and hub were built under their own supervision, and the armed predicates fired on the sessions building them. The fires, the false fires, and the same-day fixes are part of the record. |
| **A long-running computer-vision research program** (egocentric person understanding) | The primary long-running domain workload and the origin of the founding grievance. Supplied the adversarial review corpus the failure-mode characterization is mined from, and the observed natural program for the measured review-seat rounds — where independent reviews caught a scoring-region fork, an eroding metric, and a mechanism claim refuted by its own fields, with every corrected number moving against the author. |
| **A frozen predecessor vision system** | The comparison/reference oracle: migration seams and byte-identity obligations against it generated several of the most recurrent review failure modes (unverified byte-identity, frozen-reach discipline, carried oracle numbers). |
| **Two locally operated recording/automation services** | Operational custody lessons: a recorder unit that restarted thousands of times while its service state read healthy, and an outage that read as a clean skip in its own store — both found by a human, both now deterministic health predicates ("enabled is not running"; "a recorder records its own outage"). |
| **This repository** | The public projection and documentation workload — itself reviewed by the same two-seat adversarial process, which returned one of its drafts for deleting a machine-checked standard and caught the surface misdescribing its own gates. |

For each workload, the same four questions bound what is claimed: what
kind of work occurred; what the instrument observed; what was learned; and
what is **not** attributable to the instrument. Nothing on this page is a
causal-benefit claim — the observed catches and fixes are descriptive
findings whose counterfactuals were not run. What remains private stays
private: task details, code, media, transcripts, and operator commentary
do not cross; the anchors resolve the claims for an authorized reviewer.
