# What support enables

> **Status:** public funding brief
> **As of:** 2026-08-08
> **Authority note:** The private record is authoritative; this repository is the legible surface.

BrainCtx does not need funding to invent the next experiment. The powered
ablation04 apparatus is built, fixture-tested, pinned, and ready for an
admitted run. Support turns serialized, single-context research into faster,
broader, independently replicated evidence. (Anchors: `ABL04-POWER-01`,
`BUDGET-01`)

## The immediate package

The powered study contains six conditions and 192 agent runs. It isolates:

- whether an organizational knowledge store is available;
- whether there is no request path, a visible registered-only path, or a live
  serviced team; and
- whether availability changes fabrication, consultation, activation, task
  behavior, or the complete ask-to-outcome chain.

The projected inference bill is about $205–$626. The schedule is larger than
the bill: roughly 80 serialized actor-hours plus about 50.6 serialized
evaluator-hours on available hardware. (Anchor: `BUDGET-01`)

## What funding buys

### Speed

Parallel provider capacity can reduce roughly 80 serialized actor-hours to
about a day, subject to the pinned schedule and service limits.

### Generalization

Repeating the study on another independent source recording tests whether a
result is specific to one private visual sequence. This doubles the study
counts while preserving the same public claim boundary.

### Replication

Repeating the study with another actor model tests whether the observed
behavior is model-specific. Combining another recording and another model
moves total compute into the low thousands of dollars, still modest relative
to the design, evaluation, and analysis work.

### Research time and public evidence

Support also funds the people-time needed to operate the live seats, inspect
instrument behavior, retain both scoring layers, analyze nulls, prepare a
public result package, and coordinate independent replication. The expensive
part is careful evidence production, not raw inference. (Anchor: `FUNDING-01`)

## Next fundable work packages

1. Run and publish the pinned 192-run ablation04 study.
2. Add a second independent source recording for generalization.
3. Replicate at a second actor model.
4. Complete and pilot the ablation05 expectation/cascade design.
5. Package the query-free activation and live-service instruments for an
   external replication partner.

No current result establishes general coding improvement, commercial return,
or that the present team structure is optimal. Funding buys a faster and more
general answer, including a publishable null if that is what the study finds.
