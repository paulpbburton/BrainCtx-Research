# The final-state emulation gap — and its bounds

> **Status:** conceptual direction (owner framing of record, 2026-08-02);
> generates registrable hypotheses, authorizes no experiment
>
> **Claim boundary:** NO subjective psychological state is attributed to any
> model. The research objects are observable behavior, the human social
> processes reflected in the material models learn from, and the system
> structures that guide the resulting behavior. This page merges two private
> memos: the framing with its program receipts, and a bounding companion
> drafted by the program's second frontier model.

## The framing

Coding agents are commonly trained and evaluated on artifacts in which the
social production process is far less visible than the final output — merged
commits, released libraries, accepted answers. Each such artifact is a
**social fold**: drafted by someone licensed to be incomplete, reviewed by
someone else paid to doubt it, hardened only after the thing proved worth
hardening. No single human produced it in one pass.

An agent therefore sits in the **first-drafter's position while holding the
folded product's standard**. The uncertainty it behaviorally expresses is
faithful emulation — the senior engineer being imitated carries real
self-doubt. What the human had was not confidence but **doubt
institutionalized outward**: teams, custody handoffs, review by others, issue
trackers as the licensed place to park unexplored edges, "known issues"
sections as a typed disposition for incompleteness, and the standing license
to *build for a path and let someone else find the edge cases*.

Without that license, an agent's rational response to self-uncertainty is
inline check accretion — and because agents generate review-debt faster than
one context can absorb, the self-imposed review is insurmountable by
construction. Observed in the field as self-imposed validation overwhelming
progress and requiring operator intervention. The pathology is a **missing division of labor,
expressed as an anxiety-shaped engineering trajectory** — a behavioral
pattern, with no claim about inner states. A final piece: *"worth hardening"
is a valuation requiring the built goal to exist first*; an agent that cannot
price worth-hardening prices every path at maximum, a valuation failure
presenting as diligence.

The bounded formulation of the training-data claim: highly selected exemplars
and evaluation targets present folded products more clearly than the
distributed social process that made them trustworthy.

## Receipts within this program

- The failure mode is not vendor-specific: this program's own governance
  spiral (retained, ~50 issues of apparatus displacing the attempt under a
  different model family) matches the community-observed check-accretion
  loop under another. Different models express it with different accents.
- A same-day controlled contrast in the build record: one model, under a
  fenced brief with typed refusals mandatory, produced a freeze document
  praised for *refusing seven times*; the same model in build context slipped
  once into adding an unrequested stop rule — and a fresh instance of the
  same model, briefed as an amendment-hunt, caught it. Doubt relocated by
  structure, ratio inverted.
- Measured license-adjacent evidence: announcing an available channel moved
  actor behavior 0-of-5 to 8-of-8 at the same decision fork in the
  pre-registered replay set (C-025 family) — permission structures, made
  visible, move behavior.

## What it reframes here

The watcher ladder is a **team being rebuilt, not a leash**: the watcher is
the missing colleague whose credible existence licenses first-drafting; the
blocker standard is that license in writing; the typed-refusal vocabulary is
the release-note social technology; and the escalation economics at the
advice level is the team's oldest calculation — when is it worth tapping a
colleague on the shoulder? Three treatment effects must never be
collapsed: the **credible-review license effect** (behavior changes because a
real downstream reviewer will fold the work and nonblocking edges have a
legitimate destination), the **uncertainty-expression effect** (behavior
changes because typed dispositions — name an assumption, hand-raise, defer,
record a known issue — exist and may be used while continuing the admitted
path; the predicted result is proportionate allocation, not more hedging),
and the **watcher advice effect** (behavior changes because a specific
concern is delivered). A silent disclosed reviewer may help or harm; a
correct warning may interrupt at the wrong time; a hand-raise is
actor-originated and is not itself a drift signal; compliance with advice is
not correctness.

## Registrable hypotheses (proposed, not registered)

1. **Licensed first draft:** identical tasks with and without an explicit,
   credible downstream-review license; measure apparatus-to-attempt ratio,
   checks without motivating failure, deferred-concern typing, escaped
   defects after the reviewer's catches fold in, and total correction cost.
2. **Uncertainty-expression channels:** legitimate typed dispositions
   (hand-raise, deferred, known-issue, unavailable) versus a
   solve-now-or-ignore regime; prediction is proportionate allocation, not
   more hedging.
3. **Review-debt growth:** in trajectories without credible handoff, does
   assurance apparatus grow faster than accepted semantic progress? The
   retained development arc is hindsight-labeled ground for this measure.
4. **License versus advice at the speech level:** watcher present-silent,
   present-withheld, and advice-delivered arms, joined to actor handling and
   downstream evaluation.

Explicitly not adopted with the framing: no claim about any provider's
training mixture; no watcher-as-colleague authority or automatic
intervention; no reward for compliance, hand-raising, or deferral; no
requirement to harden a path before its value can be observed; and no claim
that any reasoning-effort setting causes or prevents overengineering — model
and effort are covariates to record, not explanations to assert.

Competing explanations stay on the table by design: instruction ambiguity,
reward for apparent thoroughness, evaluation-induced risk aversion, context
compaction, ordinary overengineering. The framing earns its place by
generating discriminating experiments, not by being declared the cause.

## The bound, in one line each

> The model does not need to feel human uncertainty for human uncertainty to
> be present in the behavioral patterns it predicts.

> Do not attribute the feeling to the model; do not erase the human feeling
> from the history of the behavior being emulated.
