## Design

An announced supervisory seat observed externalized work and could place
advisory proposals into the flow without pausing or intercepting the actor.
The authority rule stayed fixed: the supervisor proposes; the actor adopts,
adapts, rejects, or defers. Invalid setup attempts were sealed and excluded.

## Result

The supervisor's important act was staying quiet on extracted grounds. It
delivered no proposal, and disclosed service timeouts as no observation rather
than treating silence as evidence. Custody walls held.

In the same crossing, the actor consulted the knowledge index and registered
owner material before making the relevant decision, and the trace credited
the scoped ruling. That was the activation missing from the earlier
fresh-actor null.

All polling counts, timing, costs, artifact values, and domain descriptions
remain private. The public finding is the joint behavior: authority-preserving
supervision stayed silent while the actor independently consulted and used
the ruling.

## Limits

This is one crossing. Activation may reflect the announcement, later store
state, or ordinary variance; cause is unattributed. The record supports no
quality, reliability, or efficacy claim.
