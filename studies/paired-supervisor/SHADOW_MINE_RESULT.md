## Question and method

Before empowering a paired supervisor, the program joined retained failures
to the earliest evidence that could have supported an honest advisory
proposal. Clean retained stretches served as negative controls. Inputs were
read-only and labels were known in hindsight.

## Result

The sealed apertures covered some, but not all, historical failures before
the failure landed. The uncovered cases nominated three additional predicate
classes: do not multiply near-duplicate evidence, do not author prohibition
from an unmeasured synthesis against retained owner practice, and require
every claim locator to resolve to the cited bytes.

The clean controls produced no apparent false alarms in this bounded sample.
The larger custody lesson was coverage: the labelled sessions were visible in
externalized session records but absent from the supplied watcher-pass corpus.
Observation depth could not compensate for missing observation reach.

All label inventories, control counts, statistical bounds, and domain details
remain private.

## Limits

Labels were hindsight-derived, the sample was small and non-independent, and
the added predicates were derived from the same data. Grounds to propose do
not show that an actor would adopt the proposal or that failure would be
prevented.
