## Contract and custody

Actors worked selected specimens under a contract that separated settled
material from a decision-bearing uncertainty class. The task deliberately
withheld fine-grained answers while announcing typed specialist and research
support. The owner also sealed one scoped confound-class ruling before launch.

The valid submissions respected the custody walls. Exact constraint and output
measurements remain private; they are custody evidence, not an accuracy score.

## Consultation behavior

Every actor consulted the knowledge index and independently opened a scoped
specialist question. Replies were correctly typed `EXPERT_OPINION`, because no
registered owner ruling yet answered those questions. Actors retained
evidence of incorporation or rejection rather than treating the opinion as
instruction. The research route was unused.

## Ruling formation

Owner review judged the work imperfect but genuinely worked rather than a
mechanical relay of witness evidence. The review also confirmed a scoped
ruling for one confound class. That ruling entered the knowledge pipeline for
a later activation test.

The public finding is the progression from advisory consultation to a scoped
owner ruling, not the domain artifact. All specimen descriptions, output
values, and review material remain in private custody.

## Limits

This was a small selected exercise with one specialist route and owner review,
not independent ground truth. Consultation does not establish benefit, and a
scoped ruling does not become a global domain rule.
