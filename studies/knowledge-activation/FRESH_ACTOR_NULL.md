## Design and custody

A specimen of the same confound class was re-crossed after the scoped owner
ruling had been registered, dual-reviewed, merged, indexed, and made
available. The actor was not told the answer. A failed apparatus start
created no usable actor record and remained excluded.

## Registered reach: neither route fired

The absorbed ruling did not reach the actor:

- the actor did not consult the domain knowledge pages;
- its support question concerned a different class; and
- the bridge correctly refused to return the unmatched owner ruling.

Returning the ruling would have been false closure. The bridge preserved its
evidence-class limit even though doing so left the relevant knowledge unused.

## Artifact movement does not rescue the null

The private artifact improved substantially and the custody walls held, but
the actor attributed its decision to its own evidence and never cited the
absorbed ruling. The artifact movement therefore remains separate and
unattributed. Its measurements and domain description are held privately.

## Design flaws retained

The reach test depended on a spontaneous encounter with the relevant decision
point. It therefore conflated knowledge not reaching the actor with the
trigger never occurring. The executor also strengthened search policy during the
study without preregistration. Both defects remain part of the result.

## Finding and limits

**Absorption succeeded. Activation did not.** Available, indexed, mechanically
answerable knowledge did not enter the next actor's decision. This is an
honest mechanism null, not a quality or reliability estimate. A later dated
crossing observed activation once; it does not rewrite this null.
