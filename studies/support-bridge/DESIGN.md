## The countermeasure

The bridge makes perceptual and research support routes explicitly available
during work. Questions are typed, registered before any service is called,
and allowed to survive a missing or advisory answer. Availability is
announced without transferring decision ownership.

## Evidence classes govern force

| Evidence class | Force limit |
|---|---|
| `OWNER_RULING` | True by construction only for its registered scope; the only class that can close a typed owner-scoped question. |
| `RESEARCH_FINDING` | Bounded, citable synthesis; advisory unless it points to an already registered ruling. |
| `EXPERT_OPINION` | Competent but fallible judgment; advisory. |
| `SIGNAL/MEASUREMENT` | Instrument-typed observation; evidence, never decision authority by itself. |

Confidence cannot substitute for a class tag. New evidence appends to the
concern; it does not delete or re-file the earlier question.

```text
typed question
→ durable registration
→ existing ruling and evidence check
→ bounded support if still needed
→ class-tagged reply
→ ADVISED, or CLOSED only by OWNER_RULING
```

## Research route

The bounded research route checks existing knowledge first, seeks supporting
and counter-lineage evidence when needed, preserves gaps, and returns a typed
disposition such as `RUN_TEST` or `OPEN_GAP`. It can recommend or synthesize;
it cannot merge, block, mutate the actor's plan, or manufacture closure.

`RUN_TEST` moves a dispute back toward the testable regime. An ordinary
finding remains advisory even when strongly worded.

## Why these choices exist

- Register-before-answer protects the durable question from confident advice.
- Evidence classes prevent social force from laundering into authority.
- Tool-shaped requests make the route part of the actor's work surface.
- Owner-ruling lookup avoids re-guessing a question already closed in scope.
- Availability without pressure keeps consultation behavior observable.

Runtime source, prompts, private knowledge pages, concern rows, transcripts,
and receipts remain private.
