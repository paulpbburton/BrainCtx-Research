## Question

Can the current full BrainCtx graph execute one real three-rung sequential
coding chain with interpretable product inheritance, knowledge delivery,
independent evaluation, evidence-gated learning, and fresh continuation?

If it does, what knowledge handling, decision effects, artifact effects,
outcome effects, corrective burden, and resource use are observed?

## Study arrangement

The physical order is:

1. run one frozen three-rung BrainCtx full-graph condition;
2. seal its result and strict readout;
3. only then open the retained naturalistic historical sequence for
   descriptive comparison.

There is no new live inline comparison condition in this first pass.

The historical sequence and the BrainCtx condition use the same coding-model
family at the principal implementation role. They are not otherwise treated
as causally matched: prompts, context handling, orchestration, tooling,
requested effort, stochastic behavior, and surrounding conditions may differ.

## Primary mechanism outcome

The primary readout asks whether the BrainCtx condition completes its declared
three-rung vertical and what maximum consecutive rung frontier it reaches.

For each boundary, the private result will distinguish:

- product disposition;
- domain-knowledge disposition;
- process-knowledge disposition;
- fresh recovery;
- continuation or halt;
- successor release or non-release;
- and telemetry completeness.

## Knowledge-to-action outcome

Where the retained evidence permits, the result will report separately:

```text
knowledge delivered
→ actor handling or typed non-use
→ decision effect
→ artifact effect
→ independent evaluation
```

Delivery or acknowledgment alone is not counted as benefit.

## Qualification before launch

Before the pause, a synthetic, task-answer-free qualification exercised the
three-rung production interfaces and both successor boundaries. It completed
with model-free telemetry and no live provider call.

That result supports package/interface readiness only. It does not predict a
live semantic outcome.

## Current execution status

**Updated 2026-07-31.** The full-roster launch record below is retained as
history. It has been superseded as the study's leading edge by three bounded
observation runs under a reduced-custody runner, each of which attempted all
three rungs and retained a complete three-tier readout. Neither reached
certified closure, so the primary mechanism outcome above is unchanged: the
maximum consecutive closed rung remains 0. See the
[bounded observations](OBSERVATIONS.md).

The study was initially paused after admission and before invocation.
Twenty-five launches subsequently entered first-rung work. Five
completed rung 1,
completed both knowledge-store updates, released a cold successor, and entered
rung 2. The furthest completed all declared rung-2 plan items but failed
terminal verification, so rung 2 did not close. No launch crossed the second
successor boundary or entered rung 3.

Twenty-four launches have retained failure dispositions, and one early launch
has incomplete terminal controller accounting.

Provider-call accounting has not yet been reconciled for public reporting.
The retained attempts therefore establish only an observed execution frontier;
they do not supply an interpretable end-to-end result.
