The following limitations apply before any result is observed.

## Not a causal efficacy trial

The study contains one new full-graph BrainCtx run and one retained
naturalistic historical sequence. It can support mechanism, feasibility,
regression, burden, and descriptive comparison claims. It cannot establish
general expected performance improvement.

## Historical exposure

The historical work and related material informed BrainCtx development. It is
not an untouched holdout. Historical comparison is therefore descriptive and
regression-oriented.

## One live chain

One three-rung chain cannot establish run-to-run reliability, expected effect
size, or broad task generalization.

## Same family is not full matching

The principal historical and BrainCtx implementation roles use the same
coding-model family, but requested effort and other execution conditions may
differ. Hidden provider revisions, prompts, orchestration, context delivery,
tooling, and stochastic behavior are not fully matched.

## Full graph is a baseline

The current role graph is the architecture baseline. This study does not prove
that every role is necessary, minimal, or economically justified.

## Qualification is not semantic evidence

Synthetic model-free qualification establishes production-interface behavior
with controlled inputs. It is not evidence that live models will complete the
tasks, use inherited knowledge, or produce accepted artifacts.

## Environmental isolation is bounded

The private package proves specific custody, future-contract, repository-object,
and execution boundaries. It does not claim generalized filesystem or network
answer isolation for the MVP. Detailed public material is therefore withheld
while a future live actor could still enter the study.

## Missing or failed telemetry

Missing required telemetry is a study-integrity failure, not a successful or
null result. Product failure, runner failure, provider failure, contamination,
and unavailable evidence remain distinct dispositions.

## Knowledge stages remain separate

Knowledge availability, delivery, handling, decision effect, artifact effect,
and evaluated outcome effect will not be collapsed into a single “used
knowledge” label.
