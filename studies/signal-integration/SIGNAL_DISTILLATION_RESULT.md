## The failure mode

A distillation pass converted an unmeasured structural prior into a normative
prohibition against a working state specified by the owner. The owner rejected
the prohibition repeatedly. The store had dissolved a live discrepancy in
favor of one source before measuring it.

## The rule that landed

**An owner-specified state that conflicts with another source is not
immediately downgraded. The discrepancy is a measurement trigger.**

This is not deference. The store must retain both sides at their earned force,
register the test, and prevent advisory evidence from acquiring “must not”
power. Only a scoped owner ruling can close an owner-defined question.

## The owed measurement

The sealed measurement ran. Errors overlap far less across families than
within. That direction supported the specified state while preserving the
original caution; it authorized neither a production rule nor a prohibition.
All values, family structure, specimen details, and committee composition
remain in private custody.

An initial follow-up was refused because its composition would have counted
near-duplicates as independent support. The refusal became a registered,
better-controlled successor question rather than a result.

## The scope rule

A broader read-only custody sweep overturned earlier absence typings. The
public lesson is structural: **absence claims inherit the scope of the search
that made them**. File and site inventories, implementation locations, and
domain capability details remain private.

## Limits

The arbiter was not independent truth, the measurement population was small,
and owner testimony about historical success remains testimony. The finding
governs how the knowledge store handles discrepancy; it does not settle the
domain architecture.
