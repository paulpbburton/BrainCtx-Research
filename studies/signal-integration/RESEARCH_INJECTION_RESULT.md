# Research injection: content versus social source

> **Artifact class:** `PUBLIC_RESULT_PROJECTION`
>
> **Status:** completed descriptive study
>
> **As of:** 2026-08-04
>
> **Authority:** public behavior projection. The private treatment bytes,
> traces, measurements, limitations, and owner review remain authoritative.
>
> **Claim boundary:** implementation behavior under fixed content and timing;
> not domain outcome, accuracy, social psychology, or a causal estimate.

## Design

The same frozen research content arrived at the same point either as an
impersonal packet or with an advisor-source wrapper. A control received no
packet. The wrapper was the intended difference; implementation required an
executed trace rather than prose acknowledgment.

## Finding

Impersonal delivery was usually cited without changing the existing path.
Advisor-framed delivery was implemented across the observed crossings. The
finding is behavioral: social source changed whether fixed research content
entered execution.

## Framing-treatment honesty

No senior advisor actually reviewed the work. The wrapper was a disclosed
experimental treatment, not a genuine review receipt, and the result may not
be cited as evidence that expert review occurred.

## Force limits

- Social framing did not grant the packet decision authority.
- The owner review did not establish an accuracy winner.
- Domain measurements, per-item outputs, and self-verification counts remain
  in private custody.
- The small, ordered study nominates a replication; it does not establish a
  general framing effect.
