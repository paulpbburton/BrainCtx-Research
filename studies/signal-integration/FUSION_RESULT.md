## Design and disposition

The round tested an owner-specified composition hypothesis under a sealed
independence rule, with fit/holdout separation and a random null fixed before
launch.

The result was genuinely mixed. On one registered axis, composition added
nothing beyond the best single family and a broad committee overfit. On a
different diagnostic, composition was directionally favorable, but the sealed
frontier could not be computed because retained evidence did not support a
valid comparison domain.

Both arms entered the knowledge store:

- the negative arm did not become a prohibition; and
- the favorable arm did not become a capability claim.

A review round blocked the first landing because it recorded only one arm.
The corrected landing retained both.

## Honest null and successor

The follow-up refused to fabricate the missing comparison domain or silently
narrow the frontier. It returned an honest domain-structure null and left the
original seal dead by its own terms. A successor measurement received a fresh
predeclaration rather than inheriting the failed frontier.

All errors, separation values, random-null details, implementation details,
and specimen descriptions remain authoritative in private custody.

## Limits

The population was small, the arbiter was not independent truth, and the
diagnostic was exploratory. No weighting, threshold, or production admission
rule was promoted.
