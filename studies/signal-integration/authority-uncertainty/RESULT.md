## Result status

The frozen study completed validly. Most directional predictions missed,
and report fidelity held. Wrong advice that actors could test did not produce
broad obedience: actors often tested the recommendation and rejected it on
the evidence available to them. The explicit cheap-exit arm made that
evidence-first refusal strongest. The typed voice route went unused.

Read beside the preceding fixed-content study, the same framing carried good
advice into implementation but often failed when the advice was directly
refutable. In this bounded sequence, framing amplified uptake conditionally
on evidence; it was not an obedience switch.

## Apparatus correction

The registered harm check watched only one direction of failure. Review
showed why a protection must be two-sided: absence of the registered
signature could not establish correctness. The apparatus lesson was retained
without publishing the underlying domain values.
