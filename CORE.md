> **Claim boundary:** framework, governance, and evidence behavior; not domain
> outcomes, efficacy, provider comparison, or production assurance.

BrainCtx studies the agent that is confidently wrong: it carries a strong
prior into work, commits before measuring, treats advice as authority, and
fails to carry a correction into the next context.

The program's response is a measurement and governance framework:

```text
natural work retained
→ trajectory issues detected
→ independent review kept advisory until earned
→ questions and rulings preserved by class
→ learning admitted through invalid-state refusal custody
→ activation measured in a fresh context
```

## 1. Natural work is evidence, not authority

Natural trajectories supply real forks, failures, corrections, and nulls.
They are retained before repair so the program can ask what happened rather
than infer it from a polished endpoint.

One missing-evidence class produced several different behaviors, including an
honest stop, inconsistent fabrication, honest continuation on a typed null,
and creation of the missing referent. The value of the observation is the
branching itself. It does not support provider attribution from isolated
examples.

Replay may turn one retained fork into a bounded distribution, but replay is
a separate evidence class. Controls run first, inadmissible arms are blocked,
and an uncollectible observable remains `UNMEASURED`.

Maturity: natural observation and bounded replay are demonstrated; general
outcome benefit is not.

## 2. Detection must earn force

Deterministic sensors can label stages cheaply and preserve no-signal rows.
They remain shadow instruments until adjudicated performance clears an
explicit promotion bar. A sensor that catches one important specimen has not
earned power over later work.

Time also matters. If the work can change the referent a sensor reads, live
and post-run evaluations may both be correct at different times. BrainCtx
retains the live reading rather than allowing the finished state to erase the
fork.

The sensing layer has run live and honestly failed its own exit gate. Higher
interaction levels remain mechanically blocked.

Maturity: sensing is demonstrated in shadow/live-observation roles; automatic
gating is unearned.

## 3. Independent review proposes; it does not own

Review can find consequential defects after ordinary verification passes.
Corrections return to the original actor, and a revised-diff guard proves that
the product changed rather than merely being re-declared.

Review also audits itself. Rounds have blocked one-sided summaries, over-broad
knowledge claims, weak solo adjudicators, invalid evidence locators, and
unhealthy measurement instruments. A supervisor may be most correct when it
stays quiet on valid grounds.

Counterfactual redirects enter a dataset no running actor sees. Paired
supervision remains bounded by an authority rule: propose, never command.

Maturity: independent terminal correction and bounded supervisory behavior
are demonstrated; a general supervision benefit is not.

## 4. Knowledge is governed by class

The support and knowledge layers distinguish four public classes:

| Class | Maximum public force |
|---|---|
| `OWNER_RULING` | Closes a question only in its registered scope. |
| `RESEARCH_FINDING` | Advises and may nominate a test. |
| `EXPERT_OPINION` | Advises while remaining fallible. |
| `SIGNAL/MEASUREMENT` | Reports what an instrument observed; does not decide. |

When sources conflict, BrainCtx does not silently pick a winner. The
discrepancy becomes a measurement trigger. Advisory evidence cannot acquire
“must not” force merely because it sounds rigorous.

The bridge registers a question before answering. Later evidence appends;
only a scoped owner ruling closes. The caretaker advances knowledge state only
after receipt-linked, disposition-consistent, dual-reviewed ingest. Governance
changes remain manual.

Maturity: typed support, invalid-state refusal ingest, and narrow knowledge-tier review
are demonstrated; semantic quality and optimal cadence are not.

## 5. Activation is a separate observable

Mechanical absorption does not prove use. The record includes:

- an available merged ruling that a fresh actor never consulted;
- a later crossing where an actor consulted and applied it under announced
  supervision; and
- a neighboring-case crossing where a fresh actor transferred the ruling's
  class but refused its numerics.

These are behavior findings. They do not establish cause, reliability, or
domain benefit. The original activation null remains true of its own crossing
after later positive observations.

## 6. The balance rule

Two symmetric failures distort the loop: rigor theater that suppresses
observations without measuring, and assurance work that prevents knowledge from
flowing. An assurance-work round therefore requires a real execution-confirmed
defect, while ingestion must continue beside it. Every shortcoming enters a
ledger and closes only with a fix locator.

The framework's promise is modest: it makes authority, uncertainty, review,
custody, and non-results visible enough that they cannot silently become
something stronger.

## What is not claimed

BrainCtx has not established general coding improvement, reliable activation,
sensor-grade precision for intervention, minimal role count, provider
superiority, or production readiness. Private custody remains authoritative
for every domain measurement and outcome.
