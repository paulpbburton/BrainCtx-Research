BrainCtx studies the agent that is confidently wrong: it carries a strong
prior into work, commits before measuring, treats advice as authority, and
fails to carry a correction into the next context. The founding observation
came from real work: a long-running domain project was set aside because
agents kept overriding its accumulated domain knowledge with public priors.
Drift, in this program's vocabulary, is that — not mere circling.

The earlier answer was staged ablations: build knowledge conditions, run
controlled crossings, measure whether presence became activation. The
sealed result of that era ([retained under `archive/`](archive/README.md))
was its own finding: **presence is not activation** — knowledge available,
teams announced, uncertainty written in the agent's own words, and zero
voluntary consultation.

The current answer inverts the experiment: instead of constructing
conditions and waiting for activation, the program attached an instrument
to its own natural work and made every activation opportunity observable.

```text
natural work observed continuously, at bounded cost
→ deterministic detectors armed for the operator's most
  recurrent failure classes
→ a fire delivers a cited possibility — a reminder, one
  exact-action pause, a peer dialogue, or silence — never a verdict
→ the actor accepts, rejects, defers, or raises its hand,
  and the handling is receipted with typed lines
→ every false fire becomes a same-day predicate fix,
  re-exercised on the next natural occurrence
→ knowledge products and activation products retained in
  append-only custody, replayable from exact byte ranges
```

Three commitments distinguish the practice:

1. **No hard denial.** The instrument's authority ladder stops at a single
   exact-action pause. The bet under measurement is that a cited, well-timed
   possibility changes behavior where an enforced verdict would only change
   compliance.
2. **Instruments are suspects.** The program's most expensive lesson class
   is instruments that report health while measuring nothing — heartbeats
   through wedges, gates that no-op, references that resolve to nothing.
   Every paid-for lesson becomes a deterministic predicate that checks it,
   and withdrawn claims are withdrawn in writing.
3. **Nulls and corrections carry equal standing.** The readout corpus —
   including the fires that were wrong, the retrievals that missed, and the
   claims that had to be retracted — is the research product.

The vocabulary a reader needs: an **armed predicate** is a deterministic
detector evaluated on observed work; a **fire** is its measurement, never a
verdict; a **receipt** is the actor's typed disposition of a delivery; an
**anchor** (as in `THESIS-01`) resolves a public claim to a private
evidence class in [`PRIVATE_ANCHORS.md`](PRIVATE_ANCHORS.md). The measured
chain is in [`README.md`](README.md); the per-stage denominator discipline
(every transition carries its own offered population) is part of the
private methodology record, summarized in
[`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md).
