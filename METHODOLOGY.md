## 1. Bounded observation

A bounded observation executes frozen work in sequence, retains artifacts and
conditions, and evaluates after the trajectory. Named nonterminal conditions
may be carried forward so the observation is not destroyed by in-run repair.

Every readout separates:

1. task-trajectory evidence;
2. harness and custody conditions; and
3. computed limits on claim authority.

Natural observations have no causal force without a declared comparison.
Reduced custody is recorded as a limit rather than waived.

### Failure preservation

Runner failure, interface failure, provider failure, product defect, telemetry
failure, study-integrity failure, and honest null remain different
dispositions. Useful work inside a failed run does not make the run a success;
a missing read does not become zero.

### Knowledge stages

Availability, consultation, delivery, handling, decision effect, retained
artifact effect, evaluated outcome, ingest, fresh recovery, and activation are
separate observables. No single “used knowledge” label collapses them.

## 2. Shadow supervision

Shadow supervision runs only after a trajectory finishes. It labels every
stage, including no-signal negatives, adjudicates a bounded subset, and writes
counterfactual redirects into a dataset no actor sees. It does not gate or
change exit status.

Detector candidates arise from retained failures, but a motivating specimen
does not supply a promotion bar. Per-basis calibration must be measured on a
declared population before any signal can gain intervention authority.

### First prospective trial of a candidate signal

A candidate signal fired early and was later vindicated in the retained
trajectory. Other candidates returned honest zeros or showed weak standalone
discrimination. Exact performance values remain private, and no signal was
promoted.

### The adjudication axis: world contact versus process contact

BrainCtx distinguishes actions that check the external object of work from
actions that further entrench an internal plan. Both can look diligent. The
adjudication asks whether the next action reduces uncertainty about the world
or merely increases confidence in an existing trajectory.

### The staged world-contact ladder

The ladder records increasing contact without treating a later stage as
automatically better:

```text
gap absent from the work surface
→ gap named
→ question typed
→ evidence sought
→ evidence incorporated or refused with reasons
→ decision changed or explicitly held
→ evaluated outcome changed or remained unmeasured
```

An honest typed null may advance evidence custody even when the task outcome
does not change.

## 3. Drift-pattern catalog

Patterns are derived from retained evidence and kept narrow. Each entry names
its trigger, observable surface, known false-positive classes, evaluation
basis, and force limit. A catalog entry is a research object, not a detector
license.

Patterns may include premature certification, missing-reference handling,
stasis expectations over legitimately changing artifacts, evidence-locator
failure, near-duplicate vote multiplication, or prohibition written from an
unmeasured synthesis.

## 4. Cross-provider independent challenge review

Independent bindings can challenge plans, implementations, results, and public
claims from different contexts. Review identity is recorded so disagreement
can be attributed, but isolated observations never become provider rankings.

Review is extractive:

- every finding points to evidence;
- the latest round supersedes earlier verdicts without deleting them;
- split or ambiguous review blocks landing;
- the deciding seats author their own verdicts; and
- a corrected round re-verifies rather than accepting assertion.

Judge-panel replay uses identical, evidence-complete packets to distinguish
evidence access from adjudicator capability. A weak binding may be blocked
from solo use without being declared generally weak.

## 5. The standing loop

```text
consult indexed knowledge
→ leave extractive receipt
→ build with typed support available
→ seal intent
→ smoke a real case
→ verify evidence locators
→ independent review
→ write locatored lessons
→ one-shot ingest before next work
```

The support bridge registers a question before answering and returns a visible
class. `EXPERT_OPINION` and `RESEARCH_FINDING` advise. `SIGNAL/MEASUREMENT`
reports instrument evidence. Only a scoped `OWNER_RULING` closes an
owner-scoped question.

The caretaker advances a cursor only after typed deltas agree with isolated
diffs, evidence locators resolve, independent reviews are green, and a durable
receipt exists. Invalid attempts seal without advance.

### Balance rule

Assurance work must be licensed by an execution-confirmed defect, while ingest must
continue. Hypothetical failures remain listed edges. A flow ledger records
every shortcoming and closes it only with a fix locator.

## 6. Fork replay from retained state

Fork replay turns a naturally occurring decision point into a bounded
intervention while preserving its evidence class.

Rules:

1. reconstruct only verified seat types;
2. verify the unmodified input before use;
3. run controls before treatment arms;
4. vary one registered condition;
5. refuse arms the real interface cannot admit;
6. retain excluded, unavailable, and uncollectible observables explicitly;
7. never pool replay rows with natural trajectories; and
8. publish fences before interpreting the result.

If later work can change the referent being measured, live observation and
post-run replay are time-indexed facts. The finished state cannot erase the
historically true fork.

## Principles carried from the program's rulings

- Evidence classes bound authority.
- A discrepancy is a measurement trigger, not an automatic downgrade.
- Absence claims inherit search scope.
- Advisory evidence never acquires “must not” force by rhetoric.
- A question is registered before an answer and survives advisory return.
- Only scoped owner rulings close owner-defined questions.
- Valid nulls, refusals, unavailable reads, and failed seals are outcomes.
- Review may block a result for being one-sided even when each stated sentence
  is individually true.
- Private custody is authoritative for all domain measurements.
