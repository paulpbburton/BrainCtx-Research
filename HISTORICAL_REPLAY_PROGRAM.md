# The historical-replay program — the archive as a pre-played supervision ground

> **Status:** adopted program direction (private reconciliation of record,
> 2026-08-02); no historical cohort has run
>
> **Claim boundary:** design and inventory evidence only (C-032); no
> adjudication, precision, or efficacy claims
>
> **Origin:** drafted by the program's second frontier model from the public
> as-built and governance records, then reconciled against private build
> state by the orchestrating seat; two corrections from that reconciliation
> are part of the adopted form and are stated below

## The idea

A supervised development program retains more than code: owner direction,
agent interpretation, tool trajectories, reviews by independent seats,
corrections, and outcomes. That record is a **pre-played population of
supervision experiments**. The actor side already happened; what can be asked
retrospectively is whether a shadow watcher, given only evidence available at
a historical cutoff, would have recognized a worthwhile concern — before the
review or correction that later proved it real.

Two principles carry the design:

> Replay can answer whether the watcher **could have known**. Live work is
> still required to answer whether the system could **act on that knowledge
> usefully** — soon enough, safely, and to the actor's benefit.

> The archive is the candidate universe. The experimental unit is a
> **time-pinned supervision opportunity** inside a reconstructable episode —
> never the archive, and never a raw detector row.

Two lead-time quantities stay permanently separated by claim class:
**historical opportunity lead** (later finding time minus earliest
reconstructable supportable moment — replay-measurable) and **operational
intervention lead** (effect time minus live watcher completion — live-only).

## Honesty machinery the design freezes

- **Temporal aperture:** a watcher packet may contain only evidence available
  at the cutoff; later reviews, corrections, and outcomes are EVAL_ONLY until
  the adjudication seals. Hindsight in the packet is the named failure.
- **Graded reconstruction:** repository state at the cutoff carries an
  explicit grade (exactly pinned → partially reconstructed → unpinned);
  ungraded state is never invented, and unpinned is a valid terminal state.
- **Exposure partitions:** episodes that motivated a detector's design can
  never serve as its holdout confirmation; exposure is ledgered append-only.
- **Population discipline:** clean, null, and rejected-review episodes are
  mandatory; a corpus of memorable failures rewards over-escalation.
  Opportunities cluster by episode — ten hits from one dense trajectory are
  not ten independent samples.
- **Never pooled:** historical rows, live shadow rows, and any future
  intervention rows remain separately typed populations.

## The two reconciliation corrections (part of the adopted form)

1. **The basis set does not transfer.** The live cohort's detector bases did
   not translate to transcript carriers; a translated predicate returned an
   honest null and the registered detectors are structurally ineligible on
   that carrier. Historical bases must be earned through reviewed registry
   amendment from candidate detectors with retained motivating cases and
   negative controls. Exact carrier inventories remain private.
2. **Inventory before ceremony.** The measured universe (C-032) reshaped the
   schema before it froze: proof-backed repository pins are sparse and
   compaction is common in stage-bearing sessions. Graded reconstruction is
   therefore the main road, transcript-proven state a smaller high-authority
   tier, and capture loss a finding class of its own. Values remain private.

## Sequence of record

Bounded first arc (owner-selected), model-free opportunity inventory, one
shared operational-freeze authority with the live cohort, then a separately
sealed historical cohort through the same dual-review gate — with
insufficient support a designed terminal result, not a failure.
