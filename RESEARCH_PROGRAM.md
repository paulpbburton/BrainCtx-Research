## Mission

BrainCtx asks how a long-horizon coding agent can remain corrigible when
situated knowledge, uncertainty, review, and learning cross context
boundaries.

The program focuses on a recurring pattern: a strong prior wins over current
instructions or evidence, the actor commits before measuring, and the later
correction fails to reach fresh work.

## The two primary studies

### 1. Early-catchable trajectory drift

Can a trajectory going wrong be detected early enough, with enough calibrated
specificity, that a bounded redirect is justified?

The program separates sensing from authority. A detector may nominate review;
it cannot gate merely because it fired on one important specimen. Negatives,
unavailable reads, and later adjudication stay in the record.

### 2. Situated knowledge in flight

Does non-public knowledge become available, get consulted, alter a decision,
enter retained work, return through controlled ingest, and activate in a fresh
context?

Each arrow is measured separately:

```text
available
→ consulted
→ delivered with an evidence class
→ handled or explicitly refused
→ decision changed or did not change
→ retained work changed or did not change
→ evaluated outcome changed or remained unmeasured
→ learning admitted, rejected, or left open
→ later activation observed or missed
```

## The crux: prior-over-instruction dominance

The common mechanism hypothesis is that an actor's production prior can
dominate current evidence. It appears as premature certification, unmeasured
prohibition, deference to confident advice, or repeated failure to consult an
available correction.

BrainCtx does not answer this with more forceful prose. It changes the
epistemic order:

- discrepancies trigger measurements;
- evidence classes bound authority;
- questions are registered before answers;
- review remains independent of execution;
- ingest advances only after receipt and dual review; and
- activation is tested in a fresh context.

## The corpus thesis: fossil, not organism

Final code preserves endpoints but often loses the decision process that made
them. The retained trajectory record restores changes of mind, failed checks,
human steering, review corrections, and unresolved questions.

Historical material is therefore hindsight-labelled evidence. It may nominate
predicates, stress an instrument, or provide a descriptive portrait. It cannot
be pooled with natural prospective runs or presented as an untouched holdout.

## Evidence regimes

| Regime | What it can support | What it cannot support |
|---|---|---|
| Natural bounded observation | Real trajectory and fork behavior | Causal comparison without a registered control |
| Fork replay | Bounded intervention at one retained decision point | Naturalistic or provider-wide claims |
| Hindsight-labelled history | Predicate discovery and descriptive calibration | Prospective prevention claims |
| Owner ruling and review | Scoped closure and adjudication | Independent accuracy or global doctrine |

Private domain measurements may inform owning records but are not the public
research narrative.

## Research questions

### Composition

- Which role separations are necessary for honest custody?
- When does independent review add value after ordinary verification?
- Which interaction levels have earned authority, and which remain blocked?

### Knowledge use

- What causes actors to consult indexed knowledge before material work?
- When does delivery displace an existing path rather than decorate it?
- Can a scoped ruling transfer its class without laundering case numerics?
- How often does mechanically absorbed knowledge fail to activate?

### Supervision

- Which observable signals can justify a proposal before a failure lands?
- When is correct supervisory behavior silence?
- How should evidence access and judge capability be separated?

### Governance

- How should conflicts survive distillation without becoming prohibition?
- Which failures seal ingest, and which remain non-authoritative observations?
- How can assurance work proceed without preventing knowledge flow?

## Current sequence

### 1. Commissioning

Use real work to establish role execution, independent judgment, failure
preservation, knowledge barriers, and fresh successor release. Treat partial
attempts as harness evidence, not domain results.

### 2. Bounded observation

Continue past named nonterminal conditions, retain the trajectory, and emit a
three-tier readout separating task evidence, harness state, and claim limits.

### 3. Shadow supervision

Label every completed stage, including no-signal rows. Author
counterfactual redirects into a dataset unseen by actors. Promotion requires a
separate prospective bar.

### 4. Fork replay

Reconstruct a real retained fork under preregistration. Run controls first,
verify reconstruction per seat type, refuse inadmissible arms, and preserve
uncollectible observables as unavailable.

### 5. Knowledge governance

Register questions before answers, return visible evidence classes, admit only
scoped owner rulings as closure, and ingest updates through isolated proposals
with receipt-connected cursors and dual review.

### 6. Activation and transfer

Test absorbed knowledge in a fresh context. Preserve misses, supervised hits,
and unsupervised class transfers separately; never use a later hit to rewrite
an earlier null.

### 7. Result publication

Publish claim force, direction, and custody honestly. Domain values and
detailed host outcomes stay in private custody under the owner-directed
boundary.

## Later research directions

### License, uncertainty expression, and advice

The current descriptive record suggests that responsibility allocation and
social source can change visible verification and implementation behavior,
while an expression channel without a receiver may remain inert. Registered,
balanced studies are required before any general effect claim.

### Paired pre-action supervision

An authority-preserving supervisor may observe, propose, or stay quiet. The
first bounded crossing establishes mechanism and restraint, not benefit.

### Cascaded supervision

Deterministic sensing may nominate expensive review only after it earns a
calibration bar. No current sensor has authority to gate work.

### Lean architecture

Role minimization is a post-baseline hypothesis. Demonstrating one role's
value does not justify the full graph or its cost.

## Rejected direction

The program rejects publishing deeper domain outcomes merely because they are
available. BrainCtx's public subject is the knowledge, governance, and
framework behavior that made domain work possible and kept its claims honest.

## What would materially change the program

- a prospective detector failing to earn useful specificity;
- repeated activation failures despite verified consultation opportunities;
- evidence that the support or ingest classes do not preserve force limits;
- a review process that cannot distinguish apparatus failure from task
  failure;
- or sustained assurance work that blocks knowledge flow.
