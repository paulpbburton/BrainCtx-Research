## The night's arc

The night began with an apparent treatment result and turned into a lesson in
evidence authority. The existing scoring target was a useful graded witness,
but actors had disputed parts of it. Scoring the witness as truth converted
their disagreements into errors.

The owner supplied and ratified a replacement reference construction. Settled
agreement and explicit uncertainty became different evidence classes, and the
earlier witness stayed in the record without retaining truth force. Rescoring
changed the direction of the earlier comparison. The important public event
is not the new value; it is that a used reference was corrected rather than
defended because it already existed.

A fresh-material probe then asked whether the construction remained
reviewable outside the original cases. It did, directionally, but only as
agreement evidence. Shared witnesses can share error, so the owner ruling did
not become an accuracy claim.

The program next moved from *what counts as uncertainty?* to *what does
knowledge do when delivered?* The same advisory packet arrived before
planning, after planning, or not at all. Before planning it changed the path
and elicited bounded questions; after planning it was often acknowledged
without changing the path. Knowledge displaced early and mostly decorated
late.

Timing was not the only mechanism. A later study held research content and
delivery fixed while changing its social source. Advisor-framed bytes entered
execution more readily than impersonal bytes. The wrapper was an experimental
persona, not a true review receipt, and the public record says so.

That made the next question unavoidable: would a trusted frame still carry a
recommendation when the actor could test and reject it? The authority study
was preregistered, amended before launch, and published with its treatment
bytes sealed while the result remained unknown.

The sequence put existing pieces in an epistemic order:

```text
evidence before verdict
→ uncertainty before scoring
→ delivery before attribution
→ content separated from social source
→ registration before result
```

handling, and measurements remain in private custody.

## Review as standing practice

Owner review now receives an indexed, render-checked evidence handoff rather
than an unstructured pile. The handoff joins the competing evidence needed to
adjudicate each item and retains a digest for custody.

The indexes and source material stay private. A render check proves that a
review packet can be inspected; it does not establish that the underlying
domain artifact is correct.
