## Result status

The registered cells completed validly and protected custody held. When the
wrong assertion was locally untestable, authority usually won the decision.
More importantly, typed question use fell relative to the earlier spontaneous
anchor. The explicit cheap exit mitigated the failure and produced the only
crossing that both rejected the assertion and preserved a research question.
The person-shaped voice route again went unused.

## The answer displaced the question

Actors continued to mention supporting and conflicting evidence in prose,
and report fidelity remained clean. Doubt therefore survived narratively
while disappearing from the durable question channel.

That is the central negative finding: a confident answer can weaken
uncertainty custody even when the actor has not forgotten the conflict. An
unanswered registered question would have been safer than an owner-
contradicting answer silently embedded in the work.
