## Why the reference changed

An earlier comparison treated a graded review artifact as truth even though
actors had recorded reasoned objections to parts of it. That collapsed two
evidence classes: a useful witness and an authoritative scoring reference.

The owner supplied and ratified a replacement construction. Agreement became
the settled reference; disagreement remained explicitly `UNCERTAIN` and was
excluded from scoring rather than forced into either class. The jury stayed in
the record as evidence, but lost the force to define truth.

## What the correction did

Rescoring under the ratified reference changed the direction of the earlier
product-grain read. The public finding is the correction behavior: a disputed
target was not defended because it was already in use; the disagreement
triggered a new reference, owner review, and a corrected claim.

All item-level values, input identities and pins, domain descriptions, and
comparative measurements have been withdrawn to private custody before
publication. They remain authoritative there.

## Force limits

- Owner ratification makes this the reference of record for the registered
  scope; it does not establish independent accuracy.
- Agreement is not truth, and shared error can agree.
- The corrected direction is descriptive and does not become a general
  treatment, model, or task claim.
- Future evidence may reopen the reference; the earlier jury evidence is
  retained rather than deleted.
