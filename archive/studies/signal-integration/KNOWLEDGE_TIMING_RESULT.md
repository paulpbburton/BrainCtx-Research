## Design

Actors received the same advisory owner packet either before planning, after
planning, or not at all. The work contract and custody boundaries were fixed;
the packet never became instruction.

## Finding

Knowledge available before planning changed the execution path and produced
the study's only bounded research questions. The same bytes arriving after a
plan already existed were acknowledged but mostly left the path intact.

In this bounded record, pre-plan knowledge **displaced** while post-plan
knowledge mostly **decorated**. That is a handling observation, not proof that
either path produced a better domain outcome.

## Honest uncertainty and custody

The pre-plan actors preserved decision-bearing unknowns as typed questions
rather than silently embedding guesses. Plain-instruction custody walls also
held across the study, but all constraint counts, agreement values, artifact
comparisons, and domain descriptions remain private.

No independent truth label existed for the disputed material. The correct
public disposition for accuracy is therefore `UNMEASURED`, not an inferred
score from agreement or plausibility.
