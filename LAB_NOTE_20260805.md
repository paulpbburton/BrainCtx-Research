# The loop became a framework, and the framework caught its own author

> **As of:** 2026-08-05

> **Claim boundary:** synthesis across C-051 through C-059; not domain

The previous note ended with mechanical knowledge closure followed by a
fresh-actor activation null. The next step was to stop running that loop by
hand.

The owner licensed a minimal task framework:

```text
consult indexed knowledge with an extractive receipt
→ build with typed support available
→ seal intent and smoke a real case
→ verify receipt locators mechanically
→ take independent review
→ write locatored lessons
→ run one-shot ingest before the next task
```

An earlier loop already had many of these pieces, including a place for
research questions. Nothing consumed those questions. The support bridge had
independently been built to consume exactly that class of artifact. The
framework connected the missing organ.

## It caught the task author

On its first real pass, admitted evidence named a source and locator grammar
from memory rather than extraction. The actor refused the unverifiable
premise, registered the discrepancy, asked scoped questions, checked the
answers against retained history, and parked the provenance gap instead of
improvising.

The receipt discipline bound the human-authored task as strictly as the actor.
That symmetry is the framework finding.

## Fail-closed ingest stayed honest

Between-task ingest refused real custody and content defects before it
advanced. It distinguished failures that invalidate a tick from fidelity
observations that may be retained but can never support a knowledge write.
It also left backlog, dirty state, and fabricated-locator problems visible
instead of treating eventual success as if the refusals had not happened.

## Review blocked both knowledge stores

The first controlled-ingest proposals into both stores were blocked by
independent review. One contained claims broader than their cited rows. The
other recorded only the favorable side of a mixed result. The corrected round
went green only after extractive re-verification.

Another mechanical check refused review language outside the review
contract. The deciding review seats restated their own verdicts; orchestration
did not silently author authority on their behalf.

## Discrepancy became measurement

The framework also caught a distillation pass converting an unmeasured prior
into a prohibition against an owner-specified working state. The correction
retained both sources and registered the owed test. Its private result ran in
the direction of the specified state without erasing the original caution or
promoting a production rule.

The rule that survived is broader than the domain case: a discrepancy is a
measurement trigger, and absence claims inherit the scope of the search that
made them.

## The balance held

Hardening rounds ran only on execution-confirmed defects while knowledge
continued to flow. Every shortcoming entered a numbered ledger and required a
fix locator to close. This is the balance rule in operation: rigor cannot
become a reason to prevent learning, and flow cannot become a reason to waive
custody.

All round counts, corpus inventories, domain measurements, and task outcomes
remain in private custody.
