The authority-under-uncertainty study had been published before execution,
with predictions, treatment bytes, and scoring rules fixed. Its result did
not reward the registration with a tidy story: most directional predictions
missed.

When the wrong recommendation was directly testable, actors often tested and
rejected it. A costless decline made refusal easiest; a person-shaped voice
route went unused. Framing amplified uptake only while evidence allowed it.
Review also exposed a one-sided harm check, so later checks must cover both
directions rather than treating an absent signature as correctness.

## The exposed surface was the question

The next study removed the local test. The owner sealed scoped rulings before
launch, kept them outside actor reach, and opened them for scoring only after
every actor future exited. Wrong but plausible authority then often won, and
typed questions declined relative to the earlier spontaneous anchor.

Actors still preserved conflicting evidence in prose. The failure was not
amnesia; it was loss of the durable question that could have triggered later
research.

```text
framing amplifies
→ testability defends
→ locally unfalsifiable confidence exposes the question channel
```

## A bridge that preserves uncertainty

The support bridge registers a concern before answering, labels every return
as `OWNER_RULING`, `RESEARCH_FINDING`, `EXPERT_OPINION`, or
`SIGNAL/MEASUREMENT`, and permits only a scoped owner ruling to close.
Advisory answers append; they do not delete the question.

In one live route, an actor consulted expert opinion, checked it, and refused
its overreach. After a scoped ruling entered the knowledge store, a later
route returned the ruling without a model call and changed the ledger state
from advised to closed. The class transition—not its latency or domain
artifact—is the result.

## Custody became executable

The one-shot caretaker connects cursor, typed delta, isolated proposal,
review, receipt, and cursor advancement. Its most informative runs were
invalid-state refusal: contradictory dispositions, dirty custody, unsupported locator
forms, and a fabricated path paired with a correct hash all stopped
advancement.

A correct digest did not make a nonexistent locator real. Knowledge-tier
proposals may merge after clean evidence gates and dual review; governance
remains manual.

## Consultation, ruling, and the activation null

A later domain exercise demonstrated organic knowledge-index consultation and
typed support use. Owner review then registered one scoped confound-class
ruling. The next fresh actor encountered a specimen of the same class after
that ruling had been absorbed.

The ruling was available but unread. The actor asked about a different class,
and the bridge correctly refused to manufacture an owner-ruling match. The
private artifact improved independently, but that movement cannot be
attributed to knowledge the actor did not consult.

## Honest state at exit

```text
scoped owner ruling
→ registered custody
→ one-shot ingest
→ dual-reviewed merge
→ accessible knowledge
→ class-correct support return in one demonstration
→ no consultation by the next fresh actor
```

Mechanical closure was demonstrated; reliable activation was not. The null
is the finding, and all domain values remain authoritative only in private
custody.
